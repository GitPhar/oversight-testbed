# Audit of ng-p3-18: do both error terms show up again when the three entangled pairs are measured in a fresh chip job?

## In plain English

The result stands. A fresh ibm_fez job of the same 39 circuits showed both error terms on 2 of the 3 qubit pairs (h088 and h268). That is the pre-registered pass, and it is unreplicated. On h221 only the blur-type term came back. I rebuilt everything I could from the archived counts and refitted the models with more and wider starting points. I also dropped each tomography setting in turn and scaled for the misfit on h088. The four "present" labels on h088 and h268 stayed far above their lines in every case, and h221's rotation term stayed below. I require three changes, none of which touches the verdict. First, the write-up implies IBM's calibration changed between preparation and the run, but every property value read was identical and only the date stamp moved. Second and third, it leaves out two things the pre-registration said would be reported in every case: the Bonferroni comparison and the bootstrap nulls' maxima. What would break the pass is a third job, or a readout model that absorbs the same counts without either term. That second check is P3-17's question on the archive.

## The case that this result is false

The pass rests on exactly two pairs, so one label flip on either sinks it. Both pairs could be carried by something the 16-knob model is not built to see. The design treats the assignment matrix A as known, but in this job A comes from 1,000 shots per prepared state, a quarter of 3 November's. The bootstrap never resamples A, so its sampling error is absorbed into whichever term fits it best and counted as "chip error". Readout drift during the job is the known out-of-family rival (P3-5c audit, round 2; P3-17). It can look like blur, and a fixed per-qubit readout offset beyond A can look like a rotation in some settings. On h088 the full model itself misfits (deviance 34.2 on 11 df, about 3 times dispersed), so its parametric null is drawn from a model the counts reject, and its p = 0.001 is not calibrated. The fits are local optimisations of periodic angle parameters from starts within ±0.3 rad, so an under-fitted no-rotation or no-blur model would inflate the LR. If either h268's blur LR (38.5, the smallest present one) or h088's blur LR (84.0, but only 27 after misfit scaling) is partly an optimiser or readout artefact, the "2 of 3" is really "1 of 3", and the result is a fail. The rotation-type LRs on h088 and h268 are also larger at 2,000 shots than 3 November's were at 4,000 (250.8 vs 183.6; 271.5 vs 237.6). So whatever is being fitted changed between the jobs, which is hard to call "the same reading held". Finally, the write-up reports a secondary analysis "under the reference prepared before submission" as if it were a calibration-robustness check, which would make the result look sturdier than the record shows.

The checklist below tests each of these claims. Most do not survive. A's sampling noise was checked by the design review under W_neither (rotation exceeded its line 0 of 120 times, blur 1 of 120). Wider starts move h268's blur LR from 38.5 to at worst 36.1. Single-setting drops leave it at 24.1 or more, and misfit scaling leaves h088's blur at 27.0, all against lines near 9. The last point, the secondary that is not what it appears to be, does survive, and it becomes cut 1. The readout-drift rival is not ruled out by this design, and the write-up does not claim it is ("terms in a 16-knob model, not identified mechanisms").

## What would break it next

1. **A third job (P3-18 again, about 20 QPU s, $0).** The pass rests on two pairs and one job, and the rotation LRs on h088 and h268 came back larger at 2,000 shots than at 4,000 on 3 November, so the terms are not stable in size. A third fresh job under the same rule, preferably at 4,000 / 4,000 shots (about 44 s) to separate shots from job, is the cheapest replication, and invariant 7 wants it.
2. **A readout-bounded refit of this job's counts (P3-17's design applied to `data/p3-18-raw.json`, $0, minutes of laptop time).** If a per-qubit readout knob bounded at a few times this job's calibration absorbs h268's blur LR (38.5, the smallest present one, 24.1 after dropping setting XX), the second pair drops out and the pass becomes a fail. This needs a pre-registration before anyone fits it.
3. **A null that resamples A at 1,000 shots per column (on the archive, $0).** This would put the calibration-shot uncertainty into the p-values instead of only into W_neither's false-pass rate.
