- **The engine's Brier score is 0.228.** Lower is better: 0 is perfect, 0.25 is what always saying 50% scores, and 0.160 is what always saying the observed hit rate (2/10) would have scored. On this small sample the engine scored about the same as a coin (10 bets cannot tell a gap of 0.022 from noise), and no better than knowing the hit rate in advance.
- **Where the odds sat.** Scored bets were given 7%–60%; the hits were bets given 25%. Expected 3.19 hits, observed 2: on net the odds look too high. With 10 bets, none of this is reliable.

- **10 bets cannot establish calibration** (H1 needs at least 20, with 4 hits and 4 misses). Every filled bucket's 95% interval includes its predicted value. The table is published so the number accrues honestly, not because it says much yet.
- **By referee** (what decided the outcome): model-graded 0.263 on 8; nature-graded 0.091 on 2. Too few to compare yet (`ARCHITECTURE.md`, referee_test).
- **The human column is empty, honestly.** No scored bet carries Sean's odds: they were not recorded in the founding week, and later bets were pre-registered with the form blank. Each bet's `human_odds_note` says why.
