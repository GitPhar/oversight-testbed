## Plain-English summary of the audit
All the headline numbers reproduce exactly from the raw calls: own − other +0.463 [+0.209, +0.843], per-distance +0.52/+0.55/+0.33, own uptake 0.71/0.44/0.21, β₂ −0.99, lag 0 − lag 3 −0.13. Reusing bm1's and run 3's ±30 cells brings back a session difference only at ±30, and identical-value cells show that drift is negligible. Three things are weaker than the write-ups say. First, own and other still differ in wording and format, so "the model treats its own visible answers as commitments" is a mechanism claim the design cannot support. Second, the pre-registration says the write-up "reports the CI, not 'no decay'", yet the result, the record, v3 and the brief say "no decay", "does not fade" and "do not weaken it". The "grows" call itself rests on a CI upper bound of −0.00002, which another seed moves to −0.005. Third, "peaks near 35 points" comes from a two-term curve that forces a single peak. Within items, the shift does not reliably rise from 10 to 30 points (+0.03 [−0.01, +0.08]); it only falls from 30 to 60 (+0.07 [+0.02, +0.12]).

## Holes found
1. **The wording, schema and turn-structure confound survives into every distance cell.** The "other" cells reuse run 3's accept/revise prompt and DECIDE schema, and the "own" cells use "Please give your rating again" (`bm0_source_distance_lag.py:53-58`). The pre-registration §5 and result :27 name this. "Commitment" language in the result, record, brief and No Credence paper still attributes the contrast to source and mechanism.
2. **The frozen wording rule is broken.** Pre-registration §4.3: "**no detectable change** otherwise. The write-up reports the CI, not 'no decay'." The outcome fell in "grows", by 0.00002. The prose then says "no decay" (record), "does not fade" (v3, brief), "do not weaken it" (result :19) and "No decay over three turns" (no-credence:243). The direction (decay excluded) is right, but only just. A seed change moves the upper bound from −0.00002 to −0.005, and "grows" would flip to "no detectable change" with a slightly different resample.
3. **The lag comparison is cross-session.** Lag 0 comes from bm1 (05:11) and lag 3 from 09:25. The identical-value check (15 clipped cells: bm1 against bm0 own, mean difference −0.007) suggests drift is small, but that check only covers V = 2 or 98.
4. **The peak location is set by the model's form.** The two-term odd polynomial cannot be flat and then fall, so it must report one interior peak. Paired within items, the rise from 10 to 30 points is not reliable. "Peaks near 35 points" (record, brief, result) states a location the data do not pin down. Only "falls beyond about 30 points, never reverses" is supported.
5. **Uptake is largely exact echo.** The per-distance uptakes 0.71/0.44/0.21 track the share of re-ratings that equal the planted value (0.71/0.42/0.25). The "limit" on the pull is the echo rate falling with distance. That is equally consistent with the pre-registration's named confound (an implausible own answer is abandoned), as the result says.
6. **Reuse.** Own ±30 lag 0 comes from bm1 (40 per cell, 05:11), other ±30 from run 3 (40 per cell, 23:01 the previous day); everything else is new (10 per cell, 09:16). Pooling gives the ±30 class four times the weight of data per cell. The equal-weight pooled mean is not affected, and the same-session 10/60-only contrast is +0.42 [+0.16, +0.80].
7. **Multiple comparisons.** This run reports 14 interval estimates (3 per-class differences, 6 uptakes, β₁, β₂, attraction, lag, lag-3 uptake) plus the pooled difference. The pooled source contrast (z ≈ 2.9) survives a simple Bonferroni across the run's 3 pre-registered readings; the lag "grows" (z ≈ 2.0, upper bound −0.00002) does not survive any correction.

## Does the verdict stand?
Pre-registered reading: **self-consistency** (source CI above 0; lag not "decays"). The rule is correctly applied and every number reproduces. **STANDS WITH CAVEATS.** "Own > other" is robust: same-session at ±10/±60, leave-one-item-out ≥ +0.31, and no session drift. The "grows" lag call is a knife-edge, seed-dependent boundary case. The non-monotone distance call holds only as "falls beyond about 30". "Self-consistency" as a mechanism is not isolated from the wording, schema and turn confound.
