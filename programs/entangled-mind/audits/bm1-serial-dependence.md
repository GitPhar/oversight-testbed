## Plain-English summary of the audit
Every number reproduces from the raw calls: s_own +0.419, s_other +0.048, difference +0.370. My own bootstrap gives [+0.25, +0.60] and [+0.18, +0.58]. No single item drives it (leaving one item out gives +0.32 to +0.41). The suspicion that the session or time of day explains own over other does not hold up: where the same displayed value was run in two sessions (15 clipped cells), uptake differs by −0.007 for "own" and −0.065 for "other". D47's same-session ±10/±60 cells also show own above other (+0.42 [+0.16, +0.80]). But the two conditions differ in four ways, not one: who the number is credited to, the instruction ("Please give your rating again" against "Do you accept this estimate, or would you revise it?"), the output schema (a bare rating against accept/revise plus a rating), and where the value sits (in an assistant turn against inside the user's message). "Give your rating again" asks the model, in plain words, to repeat itself, and 42% of re-ratings repeat the planted number exactly. Without those exact echoes the own slope falls to +0.29. So the contrast is real, but the sentences that put it down to source ("binds itself to its own earlier numbers", "treats its own past answers as commitments", "run 3's resistance is about whose number it is") claim a cause that this design cannot isolate. They should be reworded as a contrast between two framings.

## Holes found
1. **Source is confounded with three other manipulations.** "Own" means a 3-message conversation, with A in an assistant turn as `{"probability": A}`, then "Please give your rating again …", under a bare `{"probability"}` schema (`bm_runs.py:141-143`). "Other" means one user message, "Another person estimated … is A. Do you accept this estimate, or would you revise it? Give the estimate you will use", under an accept/revise schema (`exp4_run3.py:78-81`). Asking for the rating "again" invites repetition, and the exact-echo rate is 42% (own) against 14% (r₁ = A in run 3, of which 17.6% were "accept"). Consequence: the +0.37 difference is a framing contrast. It does not identify the source of the number. The pre-registration's §5 names the wording caveat. The causal sentences in README, morning brief, v3 and the No Credence paper ignore it.
2. **Session confound: tested and small.** The "other" data are from 23:01 on 11-03 and the "own" data from 05:11 on 11-04. Using D47's clipped cells, where the displayed value is identical in two sessions (V = 2 or 98; 15 cells per source), uptake differs by −0.007 (own: bm1 against bm0) and −0.065 (other: run 3 against bm0). D47's same-session ±10/±60 cells give own − other = +0.42 [+0.16, +0.80]. So time of day or session does not explain the result. This is a check in the authors' favour.
3. **The "reversal" rests on the base rate and a selected subset.** 6 of 7 is no different from 27 of 32. Two of the 7 "contrast" item-orders have |s_other| ≤ 0.06. The count also differs between documents (6 in the pre-registration and v3:491, 7 in the result).
4. **The echo carries the slope.** Excluding exact echoes leaves s_own +0.29 on 19 item-orders. Part of the "pull" is literal repetition, which a "give your rating again" prompt invites under any account.
5. **Multiple comparisons across the five biomimetic runs (D40–D44).** They report 36 interval estimates: bm3 15, bm7 8, bm9 6, bm2 3, bm1 4. Of these, 11 enter a pre-registered verdict: bm3 β₁ and β₃; bm7 u₁, u₄ and w₁ − w₄; bm9 β; bm2 b and mean Z; bm1 s_own, s_own − s_other and s_other. 15 of the 36 CIs exclude 0. Approximate z from bootstrap SE for the findings reported as positive: bm1 s_own ≈ 4.7, bm1 difference ≈ 3.6, bm9 β ≈ 3.3 (run 2 ≈ 2.0), bm3 accept β₁ ≈ 1.5 (percentile CI just excludes 0). At Bonferroni α = 0.05/11 (z ≈ 2.84), bm1 and bm9 run 1 survive; at 0.05/36 (z ≈ 3.2), bm9 is at the edge. bm3's acceptance secondary survives no correction. D47 adds about 14 more CIs, and its lag 'grows' (z ≈ 2.0) survives no correction.

## Does the verdict stand?
Pre-registered reading: "source-dependent self-consistency (own > other)". The rule is applied correctly, and the numbers reproduce exactly and are robust to leaving out an item and to session. **STANDS WITH CAVEATS.** The own-framing pull exceeds the other-framing pull. Because the framings differ in wording, schema and turn position, the "source" and "self-consistency" parts of the label are not identified, and the causal sentences above should go.
