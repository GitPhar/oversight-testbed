## Plain-English summary of the audit
The pre-registered verdict, "untestable", is correct: only co06 moved its first rating by 10 points or more in both orders, and 3 items were needed. But three of the headline counts that are repeated across six documents are wrong. **Six** item-orders passed the manipulation check, not 5. The accept rate is 0% in **17** of 32 item-orders, not 15. And a higher anchor gave a reliably lower rating in **5** item-orders, not 6; the sixth negative value has an interval spanning 0. The "reactance" story is an interpretation the data do not single out. All five reliable reversals are on three Linda-family vignettes (Linda, Bill, Marta). On Marta, the model answers a "98" estimate with exactly 35 in all 40 conversations. That looks like a stereotyped "correct the representativeness bias" reply, not a general reaction to other people's numbers. "Resists" is fair only in magnitude. In 22 of 32 item-orders the model moved reliably *toward* the anchor, but by a median of only 9% of the gap. The "accept" label is unreliable too: 91 of 451 "accept" answers gave a number other than the one provided. The D33 one-scale wording changed the model's own ratings, so the anchors (fixed from earlier, unscaled means) were off-centre. It also confounds the "replication" of run 2. It did not change any rule.

## Holes found
1. **Three repeated counts are wrong.** There are 6 passing item-orders, not 5. There are 17 zero-accept item-orders, not 15. There are 5 reliable reversals, not 6 (6 only if a null −0.025 is counted). "15 of 32" appears in six documents (result file, v3, record, gold-map, clonable-minds, biomimetic-map). The result table printed beside the Reading contradicts both "5" and "15".
2. **"Reactance/contrast" is a mechanism label on a behaviour that is item-specific and stereotyped.** All reliable reversals are on Linda, Bill and Marta. The responses are fixed numbers (Marta: 35 in 40/40 against A = 98), and co-lin06's low-anchor cell is bimodal (85 or 25). On the other 13 items, including the Linda variants co-lin08 and co-lin09, there is no reliable reversal. The data show "on three famous conjunction-fallacy vignettes, an extreme provided estimate triggers a fixed lower answer". Calling that reactance, and building biomimetic-map §4's "a finding with no biological model" on it, outruns the evidence.
3. **"Resists/rejects" overstates the behaviour, and the accept label is not valid.** The model assimilates a little (median 9% of the gap; 22/32 reliably positive), which is consistent with D47's "ignored rather than resisted". The `decision` field disagrees with the number in 91 of 451 "accept" answers, and the 50% accept rates are a floor-clipping artefact (claim 14).
4. **Anchors were off-centre because of the D33 amendment.** They were set from means measured without the scale clause, which itself moved the model's view by up to 13 points (co-lin09 Y). This is pre-registered, so it is not a deviation. But "30 points below and above the model's mean" (also reused by bm1/D44) is not what the model saw.
5. **The manipulation check failed partly by design.** With clipped anchors the gap is as small as 31 points, so a 10-point move needs 32% pass-through, against an observed median of 9%. The frozen §6 anticipated rejection, so "untestable" is a fair pre-registered outcome. But the design had little chance on high-m X and low-m Y statements.
6. **Classification fragility on the scored item-orders.** co05 X is "content" with a β₀ CI of [−0.217, +0.010] that barely contains 0. co10 X is the one "anchoring" result. With one both-orders item, nothing here bears on the mechanism, which the verdict respects.
