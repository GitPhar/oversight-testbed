## Plain-English summary of the audit
Every number reproduces from the raw records with my own code: A2(κ=0), F2+, the AUROCs and the paired intervals. Two things are wrong or overstated. First, the pre-registration and the results table say 2,000 bootstrap resamples, but the code used 1,000. I reproduced the archived intervals exactly with 1,000, and with 2,000 no rule outcome changes. Second, "matches" means "the 95% interval contains 0". On Sonnet's items that is reasonably informative: F2+ recovers 1.05 [0.73, 2.50] of A2's edge. On Haiku's held-out set it is weak: F2+ recovers only 0.68 [0.20, 1.21] of A2.1's edge, so as little as a fifth may be explained. The three "positive differences" are also not independent, because they sit on the same hard-pool questions. So "every positive quantum-vs-classical difference has a classical account" overreaches. Supported: no quantum edge survives as a significant difference against F2+, the Sonnet edge is fully reproduced classically, and the held-out edges are mostly, but not demonstrably fully, reproduced.

## Holes found
1. **"Every" rests on equivalence-by-CI-including-0 and on non-independent sets.** The Sonnet result is strong (F2+ beats F2 by +0.082 [+0.047, +0.118], recovering 1.05 [0.73, 2.50] of the edge). The held-out result is weak: 0.68 [0.20, 1.21] of A2.1's edge and 0.82 [0.42, 1.64] of A2's. And the three "positive differences" are one phenomenon: the same hard-pool questions, with 21 of Sonnet's 30 errors falling in Haiku's held-out wrong set (see audits/sonnet-replication.md). "Every" counts one effect three times and treats non-detection as equivalence.
2. **Forking paths, partly mitigated.** F2+ was conceived after seeing the edge (21:03, 4.5 min after D27), and only one classical extension was pre-registered. Its parameters came from Haiku train/validation only, and Sonnet's records played no role in fitting, so the Sonnet match is a genuine out-of-sample result. But the choice of *which* classical feature to add was made with the edge in view, and outcome 1 was the only way to get a clean classical account. Had F2+ fallen short, other extensions could have followed; the archive shows no sign that any did.
3. **Addendum E code not frozen.** `heldout_f2plus.py` was first committed with its results (73 s after the pre-registration). The rule copies Addendum D, so the scope for steering is small, but "before scoring" cannot be checked.
4. **Resample count deviates from the pre-registration** (1,000 used, 2,000 stated in Addenda D and E and in sonnet-ablation.md:14). There is no change in outcome.
5. **A2.1 was not part of Addendum D on Sonnet.** On Sonnet, A2.1 − F2.1 is +0.101 [+0.029, +0.169] (my exploratory computation). It is another positive quantum-vs-classical difference that was never reported, though F2+ is within [−0.074, +0.032] of A2.1 there.
6. **Minor.** Paired "point" values in the JSON are bootstrap means. "Couplings do not matter" is true only at the fitted weak couplings; at v1 strength they hurt.
