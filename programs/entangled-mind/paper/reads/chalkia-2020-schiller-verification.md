- what_tested: Whether the influential Nature 2010 finding holds when the original data are reanalysed. The finding: extinction given within the reconsolidation window after a reminder (reactivation-extinction) prevents the return of fear, compared with plain extinction.
- on_whom: The original Schiller et al. data. Experiment 1: 65 included as reported, 84 in the full OSF sample, with alternative inclusion sets of N = 74 and N = 35. Experiment 2: an N = 22 subset that could be located.
- how_scored: Seven analysis sets. Three copy the original pipeline (differential SCR, rm-ANOVA Group x Time on spontaneous recovery and reinstatement); four apply principled exclusion rules. Frequentist tests plus JASP Bayes factors.
- result: The originally reported exclusion rate was 8%. Applying the published criteria in their own replication forced them to exclude close to 75% of participants. The original criteria applied to the original data excluded 49 of 84. Every reported group difference in fear recovery depended on qualitative experimenter inclusion decisions. With any principled exclusion rule, fear recovery was "highly similar between groups". The Experiment 2 verification on the available sample failed to confirm the reported differences. The original paper had over 600 Web of Science citations by September 2019.
- limits: This is a reanalysis, not new data. The companion registered replication (Chalkia et al. 2020, Cortex, "No persistent attenuation of fear memories in humans") is cited but was not read here (PMC7115861 fetch rate-limited). It covers fear conditioning only, not declarative reconsolidation.
- borrow: Failure mode, "analyst degrees of freedom in inclusion". For any LLM test in this queue, pre-register exclusion rules (which items, seeds, prompts or parse failures are dropped) before scoring, and report the full-sample result alongside. This is exactly what decided Schiller et al. A biomimetic "update the memory during a reactivation window" claim (for example, edit or fine-tune right after the model retrieves a fact) needs a no-reactivation arm with the same update.
- next_steps_it_suggests: Independent, pre-registered direct replications with principled exclusions and shared data.
- already_run_check: none (human; no network).
- transfers: partly — the flagship human reconsolidation-update effect does not survive principled reanalysis, so a "reconsolidation window" prediction for LLM editing or fine-tuning inherits essentially no evidential weight from the human literature.
