# Source monitoring (review and framework) — Marcia K. Johnson, Shahin Hashtroudi & D. Stephen Lindsay, 1993, Psychological Bulletin 114(1)

- field: cognitive psychology

- read_by: scout-bmq reader B, 2026-11-04
- what_tested: review. People do not retrieve an abstract source tag; they attribute a memory to a source by judging its qualities (perceptual, contextual and reflective detail) against flexible criteria. This makes attribution decision-dependent and open to error.
- on_whom: reviews human studies, including amnesic patients (Schacter et al. 1984), older adults, frontal patients and normal adults.
- how_scored: varies across the reviewed studies: source identification accuracy, old-new recognition, false fame, plagiarism counts.
- result: mostly qualitative. Findings reported: the more often subjects GENERATED an item, the more often they later thought they had SEEN it (Raye et al. 1980), and a stricter judgment criterion reduced the confusion. False fame: on an immediate test, studied non-famous names were LESS likely to be called famous; after a delay they were MORE likely, consistent with source being lost while familiarity remains. Cryptomnesia: subjects later claimed as their own exemplars that others in the group had produced, plagiarising more from those who spoke just before them. "Source amnesia" (Schacter et al. 1984): facts retrieved without memory of how they were acquired. Older adults remembered trivia facts as well as young adults after a week but lost their source. Long retention intervals are expected to increase the influence of fiction (sleeper effect).
- limits: narrative review; few effect sizes quoted in the text read; human-only.
- borrow: three direct predictions for T2 on our own-vs-other uptake effect (+0.46 own minus other): (1) delay or consolidation should strip source before content, so after context distillation the own-vs-other gap should shrink while the content's pull remains (false-fame / sleeper pattern); (2) cryptomnesia predicts misattribution TOWARD self for content that came just before the model's own turn; (3) criterion manipulations (asking for strict source judgments) should reduce confusions, which is a cheap prompt control.
- next_steps_it_suggests: specify how qualitative characteristics are encoded and weighed; relate frontal vs temporal/diencephalic damage to different source-monitoring failures.
- already_run_check: T2 (source half): the human basis. In LLMs only in-context reality monitoring has been run (Ranjan et al. 2026, separate record by reader E; no 7-8B models, no consolidation). Source after consolidation was not found.
