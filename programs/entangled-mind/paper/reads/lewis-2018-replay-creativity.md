- what_tested: A theory paper with a proposed but unimplemented network model. iOtA: when overlapping memories are replayed close together in time in slow-wave sleep, the shared elements are strengthened (Hebbian). Add forgetting of each memory's own details, and only the gist (the schema) is left. BiOtA adds REM sleep: the cortex replays schemas it has already semanticised together with randomly activated other schemas, so links form between distant schemas (analogy). Alternating NREM and REM is proposed to give ever deeper compression. A further proposal is an autoencoder-like check in REM: which hippocampal memories are already faithfully coded in cortex and so need no more NREM replay.
- on_whom: No new data. It cites human behavioral studies (statistical learning, grammar, the number-reduction task, DRM) and rat place-cell replay. The model is hypothetical (a three-layer PDP network trained Rumelhart-style); no simulation was run.
- how_scored: Not applicable; no outcome measured.
- result: No new numbers. Facts reported from the cited literature: a roughly 20 s track experience is replayed in about 200 ms in NREM; NREM replay runs 6-20 times faster than the real experience; PGO waves rise by as much as 300% in sleep after learning. On DRM gist memory, sleep has been reported to help, to suppress it, and to make no difference; one study found SWS negatively correlated with false memory, which may mean SWS strengthens context and so helps lures be rejected.
- limits: The model was never implemented or tested here. The authors themselves ask why people with no REM (patients, people on antidepressants) "often appear to have entirely normal cognition". The DRM evidence is mixed. All mechanisms stated are qualitative.
- borrow: (1) The iOtA prediction is cheap to test on an LLM: overlap plus forgetting gives gist. LoRA-train on N short "episodes" that share a hidden structure, each with idiosyncratic details. Then measure recall of each episode's details against endorsement of the shared rule or a never-seen gist lure. Prediction: as training goes on (or after a shrink-toward-base step, T4), details fall and gist rises. (2) The autoencoder "has this been consolidated yet?" check is a selection rule for T7: only replay transcript items the weights cannot yet reproduce. In our setting, consolidate only those transcript answers the model fails to reproduce with the transcript hidden. (3) The flagged mixed DRM result (SWS may strengthen CONTEXT, helping lures be rejected) is a failure mode T2 must allow for. Consolidation can keep source/context instead of stripping it, so T2 has to score both directions.
- next_steps_it_suggests: Implement the hypothetical model and test behaviorally whether REM/NREM replay affects gist abstraction and links between distant concepts. Separately, "implement offline processing stages equivalent to non-REM and REM sleep" in AI systems.
- already_run_check: none. It explicitly proposes but does not run a T3-like replay model. Nothing on LLMs.
- transfers: partly. The gist-from-overlap plus forgetting prediction and the "replay only what is not yet consolidated" rule turn into LoRA tests at 7-8B or on Qwen2.5-0.5B for $0. The REM/NREM pharmacology (ACh, PGO) has no LLM counterpart.
