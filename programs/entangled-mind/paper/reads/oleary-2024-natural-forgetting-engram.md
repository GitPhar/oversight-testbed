This is a SUBSTITUTE: Ryan & Frankland 2022 Nat Rev Neurosci (doi 10.1038/s41583-021-00548-3) is paywalled and no open copy was found. This is the same lab's empirical test of that perspective's claim (forgetting is adaptive and reversible, a matter of engram accessibility), and it cites Ryan & Frankland 2022 as its framing.
- read_by: scout-bmq reader A, 2026-11-04
- what_tested: Is natural forgetting loss of the engram, or a reversible switch of the engram to an inaccessible state driven by experience (prediction error)? Experiments: object recognition at 24 h vs 2 weeks; activity-tagged dentate-gyrus engram cells (Fos-tTA) were optogenetically silenced or activated; environmental enrichment; a brief 5-min reminder; repeated exposure to the context with no objects (a negative prediction error); a Rac1 inhibitor. A Rescorla-Wagner model was fitted with separate learning rates for negative prediction errors.
- on_whom: Mice (Fos-tTA, C57-background), n = 9-12 per group (per the figure legends).
- how_scored: Discrimination index ((novel - familiar)/(novel + familiar) exploration time); engram reactivation (eYFP+ cells that are also c-Fos+); engram spine density and volume; maximum-likelihood fits of the Rescorla-Wagner model.
- result: Memory was present at 24 h (t(9)=5.12, p<0.001) and gone at 2 weeks (t(11)=0.93, p=0.36). Engram size did not change (t(6)=0.18, p=0.86), but engram reactivation fell (t(6)=4.56, p=0.003), and reactivation correlated with discrimination (r=0.61, p=0.02). Spine density fell after forgetting (t(6)=8.23, p<0.001). Optogenetic activation of the forgotten engram restored recall (DI difference t(17)=2.14, p=0.04), "the forgotten engram was still intact". A brief reminder 1 h before test restored recall (t(14)=4.57, p=0.001) and raised engram reactivation. Six empty-context exposures sped forgetting at 1 week (t(15)=2.83, p=0.012), and silencing the engram during those exposures prevented it (t(20)=2.12, p=0.046), so forgetting by updating needs the original engram to be active. The Rac1 inhibitor prevented 2-week forgetting (t(17)=3.53, p=0.002). Fitted learning rate for negative prediction errors: alpha- = 0.07 under standard housing, 0 with enrichment, 0.01 under Rac1 inhibition.
- limits: The authors concede that the empty-context effect may reflect renewed novelty or curiosity rather than forgetting (both objects were explored more, F(3,30)=11.30). Spine density was measured only at the engram level, not split by reactivated vs non-reactivated cells. Enrichment's object-specific effect was not separated from its general cognitive effects. Only one task (object recognition) and one region (dentate gyrus).
- borrow: (1) The core LLM test is T8 phrased the way they did it. "Forget" a fact in an LLM by NEGATIVE PREDICTION ERROR: LoRA on prompts that cue the fact's context but complete without the fact, the analogue of the empty box. Then test (a) whether a linear probe or activation patch at the old site still recovers the answer (the optogenetic analogue = activation steering), and (b) whether a single brief in-context reminder restores it. Compare against facts forgotten by plain interference (training on unrelated data). Their prediction: forgetting by negative PE is reversible and needs the old representation to be active during the forgetting phase. (2) The control for (1) is to freeze or ablate the old representation during forgetting training; it should block forgetting, as engram silencing did. (3) Fitting a Rescorla-Wagner forgetting rate (alpha-) per condition gives one number to compare across LLM fine-tuning conditions.
- next_steps_it_suggests: Look at spine changes in reactivated vs non-reactivated engram cells, separate enrichment's object-specific effect from its general effect, and look at other memory types and regions.
- already_run_check: none on networks. Closest to T8 (it shows spontaneous recovery after a reminder, and recovery by direct reactivation of a forgotten trace) and to T1 (the trace stays intact while behavior is lost), but only in mice.
- transfers: partly. Optogenetics and Rac1 pharmacology are killed. The logic (forgetting as learned inaccessibility from negative prediction error; recovery by reminder or by direct reactivation; blocking forgetting by blocking the old trace's activity) maps onto LoRA-unlearning plus probe/steering tests at 7-8B for $0.
