# Continual learning of large language models: a comprehensive survey — Haizhou Shi, Zihao Xu, Hengyi Wang, Weiyi Qin, Wenyuan Wang, Yibin Wang, Zifeng Wang, Sayna Ebrahimi, Hao Wang, 2024, arXiv 2404.16789 (v3, Nov 2024; ACM Computing Surveys)

- what_tested: A survey of continual pre-training, domain-adaptive pre-training and continual fine-tuning of LLMs, with their evaluation metrics and benchmarks.
- on_whom: The published LLM continual-learning literature (no new data).
- how_scored: Narrative taxonomy. The metrics it catalogues are overall performance, forgetting/BWT, FWT, LAMA cloze probes, FUAR, and TRACE "X-Delta" (general ability, instruction following, safety).
- result: No pooled numbers. Key framing points: "instructions can be seen as natural-language-encoded task indices", so most continual instruction tuning is task-incremental (the easiest van de Ven scenario). For LLMs, the authors say storage is cheap and the real constraint is compute, which motivates efficient and selective replay. They note "anticipatory recovering" in cyclic fine-tuning as an under-explored emergent property. They flag the lack of theory and of understanding of "knowledge forgetting, transfer, and acquisition". Absent from the text (keyword checks on the v3 PDF): zero mentions of "contamination" and zero of "spurious"; "seed" appears once.
- limits: A catalogue, not a critique. It does not assess whether reported gains beat simple replay or tuned baselines, and it does not address train/test contamination or the format-vs-knowledge confound (spurious forgetting appeared after v3).
- borrow: (1) Treat the instruction or prompt template as a task label. An LLM forgetting probe that keeps the original task's instruction is Task-IL, so pre-register a cue-free or neutral-template probe as well. (2) TRACE's split into general-ability, instruction-following and safety deltas is a ready-made format-vs-knowledge-vs-alignment decomposition for scoring forgetting. (3) Because the survey itself does not police contamination or baselines, the engine must add both itself: synthetic or post-cutoff facts for knowledge items, and a plain-replay and tuned-vanilla arm.
- next_steps_it_suggests: Theory of continual LLMs; efficient replay and sample selection; controllable external memory; continual learning under custom preferences.
- already_run_check: none directly (survey). It points to the anticipatory-recovery work relevant to T6 and to selective replay (KPIG, forgetting forecasting) relevant to T7.
- transfers: partly — the evaluation vocabulary transfers. Its silence on contamination, seeds and simple baselines is itself the finding: the LLM continual-learning literature it summarises cannot be taken at face value.
