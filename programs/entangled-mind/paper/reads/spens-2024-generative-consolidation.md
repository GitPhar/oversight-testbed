# A generative model of memory construction and consolidation — Spens & Burgess, 2024, Nature Human Behaviour 8:526-543
- url: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-023-01799-z.pdf (open access; PDF fetched and converted with pdftotext; also PMC10963272)
- field: neuroscience (computational)
- read_depth: partial (abstract, Introduction, model sections "Consolidation as the training of a generative model" and "Combining conceptual and unpredictable sensory features", Results on recall, semantic memory, schema distortions, boundary extension and DRM, and the full Discussion with its testable predictions; Methods not read)
- read_by: scout-bmq reader A, 2026-11-04
- what_tested: Consolidation as teacher-student learning. A modern Hopfield network ("hippocampus") memorises events in one shot. Random noise into it reactivates stored memories ("replay"), and those replays train a VAE ("neocortex"). Does this one mechanism give the temporal gradient of hippocampal amnesia, semantic memory, imagination, relational inference, and schema-based distortion that grows with consolidation (prototype pull, boundary extension, DRM lures)?
- on_whom: Simulations only: Shapes3D (10,000 images encoded, 10,000 replayed samples), MNIST (10,000), and DRM word lists over a VAE trained on simple-story word counts.
- how_scored: Reconstruction error. Semantic decoding accuracy (SVM from latents). Within-class pixel variance before vs after recall. Object-size change for zoomed-in/out inputs. Probability of recalling the DRM lure as a function of list length.
- result: Semantic decoding accuracy rises with consolidation epochs together with reconstruction quality (rs(48) = 0.997). Recalled MNIST digits become more prototypical: within-class variation falls (paired t(7,839) = 60.523, Cohen's d = -0.684). Atypically zoomed views are pulled toward the typical view (boundary extension/contraction). DRM: lure recall is higher for longer lists (rs(10) = 0.998), as in human data. A higher encoding-error threshold stores fewer sensory details and gives more prototypical recall.
- limits: Static images, not sequences. No decay or capacity limit simulated in the hippocampal store. Backprop rather than local rules. Lifelong consolidation and catastrophic forgetting are explicitly NOT addressed. Generative replay from the student is proposed for this, and a GPT-2 sequential student is proposed as future work (later done; see spens-2026-compressive-rag-consolidation.md).
- borrow: (1) The DRM-lure-versus-list-length curve is a clean, quantitative semanticisation metric that ports to an LLM. Put DRM lists in context (the "hippocampus") vs LoRA-consolidated (the "neocortex"); prediction: lure endorsement rises after consolidation and with list length, while studied-word recall falls. (2) The "encoding error threshold" control: storing more surprising detail reduces distortion. This gives T7 a parameter to sweep (fraction of high-perplexity transcript tokens kept for replay). (3) "Context binds unique source tags (id_n); weights keep only gist" is T2's source-amnesia hypothesis stated mechanistically.
- next_steps_it_suggests: Representational-similarity tests of latent structure developing in association cortex. Lesion predictions (less boundary extension after HF/vmPFC damage). Sequence models such as GPT-2. Generative replay to prevent catastrophic forgetting.
- already_run_check: none on an LLM in this paper (VAEs only). It is the precursor to the 2026 paper, which nearly runs T2.
- transfers: partly. The VAE/Hopfield images and their vision results do not transfer and are killed. The teacher-student consolidation logic and the DRM metric transfer directly to LoRA on a 7-8B model.
