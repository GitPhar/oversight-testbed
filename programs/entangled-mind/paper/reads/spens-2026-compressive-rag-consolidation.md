- what_tested: A complementary-learning-systems model with LLMs. "Hippocampus" = a modern Hopfield store of compressed episodes (an xRAG gist vector plus the highest-perplexity phrases). "Recall" = RAG: the trace is loaded into the context ("working memory"). "Consolidation" = LoRA training of the LLM on its OWN reconstructions of the stored episodes (teacher-student). "Forgetting" = LoRA on new stories with no hippocampal reminders. Questions: does consolidation give semantic knowledge, gist-based distortion and loss of detail over time, as in human data?
- on_whom: Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.2 with LoRA (attention q/k/v/o and MLP gate/up/down) for narratives; GPT-2 for some sequence tasks. 500 ROC Stories; 50 factual questions; Bartlett's "War of the Ghosts" after pre-fine-tuning on 1000 Wikipedia articles from each of 5 categories; human comparison data from HIPPOCORPUS (n=2779 recalled, 1319 retold memories) and NFRD (4 stories, 458 transcripts).
- how_scored: Recall accuracy = cosine similarity of MiniLM embeddings (original vs recalled). Trace size in tokens. Semantic memory = fraction of 50 questions where the answer embedding is closest to the correct answer out of 3. Concreteness, richness and specificity rated by gpt-4o-mini. Word frequency. Semantic-intrusion counts as a function of temperature and epoch. Cosine distance to the background dataset.
- result: Gist plus details gives the best balance of trace size and recall accuracy (Fig. 3a: imagine-only < gist < gist+1 < gist+3 < full). Consolidation moves the neocortical reconstruction toward the encoded story. Semantic-question accuracy rises after consolidation (example: before, "Traffic delay"; after, "She overslept"). Training on 50 new memories per stage moves recall away from the story ("forgetting"), and the authors say this gradient is "steeper than would be expected of human memory". Encoded memories are less concrete, detailed and specific than the originals, and consolidated ones lower still. High-frequency words increase, in parallel with the human data. Bartlett: the consolidated story is more distorted than the encoded one ("an approximation of an approximation"), and intrusions reflect each model's Wikipedia prior (sport, health and so on), more of them at higher temperature. Exact effect sizes are in figures and Source Data, not in the text read.
- limits: The hippocampal trace is assumed to fade, not simulated. There is no meta-controller for when to replay or encode, so replay is not prioritised. Catastrophic forgetting is steeper than in humans, and "generative replay in the neocortex" is left as future work. Annotation by gpt-4o-mini. It never measures context sensitivity (order effects), source attribution, or whether the consolidated model's judgments become more or less consistent.
