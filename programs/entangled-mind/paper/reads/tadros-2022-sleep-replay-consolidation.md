# Sleep Replay Consolidation (SRC) — Tadros, Krishnan, Ramyaa & Bazhenov, 2022, Nature Communications 13:7742
- url: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC9755223/fullTextXML
- field: machine learning
- read_depth: partial (first ~100k of 153k characters of the PMC full-text XML: results incl. Tables 1-2, discussion, most of methods; supplement not read)
- read_by: scout-bmq reader C, 2026-11-04
- what_tested: After ordinary backprop training on a new task, convert the ReLU network (no biases) to a spiking network (Heaviside units, weights scaled by each layer's max activation, thresholds from a genetic-algorithm search), drive it with Poisson binary noise whose per-pixel rate is the mean intensity of ALL past training inputs (only that mean vector is stored), apply a local Hebbian rule (increase when pre fires before post; decrease when post fires without pre), then convert back. Compared with sequential training, EWC, SI, orthogonal weight modification (OWM), rehearsal with 0.75% old data, iCaRL, and combinations.
- on_whom: Toy 4-patch task; MLP 1200-1200-10 on class-incremental MNIST, Fashion-MNIST, multi-modal MNIST (2 classes per task, 10 epochs per task); CUB-200 on frozen ResNet-50 embeddings (2 tasks of 100 classes); CIFAR-10 on frozen conv features (5 tasks of 2 classes). Table 1 SDs over 5 trials. No transformers or language models.
- how_scored: Test accuracy after the full sequence; hidden-layer correlation/sparsity; task-specific neuron firing during sleep.
- result: Table 1 (final accuracy %): MNIST sequential 19.49, EWC 20.37, SI 21.38, OWM 77.04, SRC 48.47, rehearsal 79.91, rehearsal+SRC 86.47, parallel upper bound 98.02. Fashion: 19.67 / 21.39 / 22.05 / 58.35 / 41.68 / 55.19 / 67.82 / 87.86. CIFAR-10: 19.01 / 18.54 / 22.84 / 34.23 / 44.55 / 39.39 / 58.24 / 72.43. Multi-modal MNIST: SRC 61.33 vs EWC 74.55 (SRC weaker there). iCaRL K=100: 65.5 -> 78.1 with SRC; K=200: 76.9 -> 84.5. After sleep, between-class hidden correlations fall to near zero and representations get sparser; task-specific neurons fire more than random ones during sleep (layer 2: t(200) = 5.215, p < 0.001). SRC slightly hurt a well-trained single task while helping poorly trained ones. Quote: "SRC can be complementary to rehearsal methods by incorporating partial old data replay".
- limits: Authors: it works because "information about old tasks remains present in the synaptic weights"; below rehearsal methods alone; conv layers frozen; thresholds and scaling tuned per dataset; simplified REM-like sleep. Small MLP heads only. Not applied to transformers/LLMs in this paper; my searches found no SRC application to a transformer or LLM (see bazhenov-2026-not-just-after-one.md for the 2026 follow-up, still MLP/CNN).
- borrow: (a) The diagnosis "forgotten behaviour, knowledge still in weights" is precisely T1's hypothesis: SRC's success is indirect evidence that sequential fine-tuning leaves recoverable old-task structure; a linear probe on our LLM tests the same thing directly. (b) SRC's mechanism (mostly weight DECREASES, decorrelation, sparsification) makes it a sleep-like downscaling method relevant to T4. (c) Porting SRC itself to a transformer is not cheap or obvious (spiking conversion of attention/LayerNorm is undefined in the paper), so the transferable piece is the "noise-driven, label-free, local offline phase" idea.
- next_steps_it_suggests: Combine REM-like and NREM-like (slow-wave) sleep; richer sleep dynamics and learning rules; tune thresholds from biological firing rates.
- already_run_check: T4 nearly (offline, unsupervised phase that mainly weakens weights after new learning, on MLPs; not delta-to-base shrinkage, not LLM). T1 partly (shows old-task information survives in weights after behavioural forgetting, but via recovery, not via a probe). T8 partly (recovery of forgotten tasks without old data). Others: none.
- transfers: partly — the hypotheses transfer; the spiking-conversion algorithm does not transfer to transformers without new work.
