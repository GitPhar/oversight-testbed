The Window The Entangled Mind · one belief state, from encoding to collapse

What you are looking at. Each glowing point is one claim from the language model's own short explanation of its answer to the question shown, placed on a ring. Its angle around the ring is its phase (right is yes, left is no, top and bottom are neither), and its distance from the centre is its amplitude, how strongly it is held in the quantum state. Hue and brightness repeat the same two quantities. Solid lines are the relation web the claims were judged to form: green supports, red contradicts. Dashed lines appear once claims are entangled, with opacity equal to that pair's entanglement (negativity). A dotted gold ring marks a hedge, a claim the model stated with no lean. Watch where the state moves hedges. Drag the scrubber from encode through each evolve step to collapse, where the state is measured in the yes/no basis, and on to readout, where the archived runs of every arm are compared. The panel on the right shows the truth, the model's answer, and each arm's conflict score against its own pre-registered threshold. Compare the entangled arm A with the classical contradiction count E to see items where the entangling step loses a signal that plain counting keeps. Every stage here is an exact, noiseless state computed from the archived claims. The arm scores are the archived results, as run.
