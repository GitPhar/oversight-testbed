## Novelty line
Tripathi et al. 2022 (Phys. Rev. Applied 18, 024068; arXiv 2108.04530) state that on IBM fixed-frequency transmons the always-on ZZ coupling "contributes to both coherent and incoherent crosstalk errors", alongside open-system decoherence; Carignan-Dugas et al. 2024 (Quantum 8, 1367; arXiv 2303.09945) measure "substantial coherent errors biased in Z" on three IBM chips next to decoherent error; Georgopoulos et al. 2021 (Phys. Rev. A 104, 062432) find operations "more or less erroneous than the hardware-calibrated parameters show". Our finding that ibm_fez pairs need both a rotation term and a blur term beyond the calibrated simulator is the same statement in the same direction, on a tunable-coupler Heron device. ## Papers opened
- Tripathi, Chen, Khezri, Yip, Levenson-Falk, Lidar, 2022, "Suppression of crosstalk in superconducting qubits using dynamical decoupling", https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.04530. IBM cloud processors; ZZ coupling "always present" and "contributes to both coherent and incoherent crosstalk errors"; errors attributed to "open quantum system effects and spurious inter-qubit couplings". States both error kinds at once on IBM hardware.
- Carignan-Dugas, Ranu, Dreher, 2024, "Estimating Coherent Contributions to the Error Profile Using Cycle Error Reconstruction", https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.09945. ibmq_guadalupe, manila, montreal; "substantial coherent errors biased in Z" measured beside decoherent error with a twirling-based method. States coherent plus decoherent on IBM chips; abstract does not name ZZ as the source.
- Georgopoulos, Emary, Zuliani, 2021, "Modelling and Simulating the Noisy Behaviour of Near-term Quantum Computers", https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.02109. Compares a calibrated-rate noise model with a genetic-algorithm fit against an IBM machine; "certain operations can be more or less erroneous than the hardware-calibrated parameters show". Bears on calibration models mispredicting; abstract does not split coherent from incoherent.
- Hashim et al., 2021, "Randomized compiling for scalable quantum computing on a noisy superconducting quantum processor", https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.00215. Miscalibration, drift and crosstalk "can lead to a coherent form of error"; randomized compiling converts them "into stochastic noise". Superconducting (AQT, not IBM); states coherent errors present on hardware.
- Wallman, Emerson, 2016, "Noise tailoring for scalable quantum computation via randomized compiling", https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.01098. Theory of converting coherent errors into stochastic Pauli errors. Background only, no device result.
- Sheldon, Magesan, Chow, Gambetta, 2016, "Procedure for systematically tuning up crosstalk in the cross resonance gate", https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.04821. Measures the full CR Hamiltonian and cancels unwanted terms; abstract does not use "ZZ" or "coherent". Bears only indirectly (Hamiltonian error terms on IBM-style qubits).
- Mundada, Zhang, Hazard, Houck, 2019, "Suppression of Qubit Crosstalk in a Tunable Coupling Superconducting Circuit", https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04182 (Phys. Rev. Applied 12, 054023). Static ZZ cancelled by two couplers interfering. Tunable-coupler ZZ background; not IBM.
- Moueddene, Khammassi, Feld, Hamdioui, 2021, "A context-aware gate set tomography characterization of superconducting qubits", https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.09922. Cloud superconducting platform (vendor not named); "a large coherence in the errors is observed" together with crosstalk and memory effects. Bears on coherent errors found by GST on a cloud device.
- Dasgupta, Humble, 2020, "Characterizing the Stability of NISQ Devices", https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.09612. IBM Yorktown histogram stability in time and space. Does not bear on the coherent-versus-incoherent question.
- McKay, Hincks, Pritchett, Carroll, Govia, Merkel, 2023, "Benchmarking Quantum Processor Performance at Scale", https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.05933. Layer fidelity on ibm_sherbrooke (Eagle) and ibm_montecarlo (Heron); "sensitive to crosstalk"; abstract does not mention coherent errors. The only Heron paper opened.
- Ferracin et al., 2024, "Efficiently improving the performance of noisy quantum computers", https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.10672. AQT four-qubit device, error mitigation; the abstract does not say calibration simulations miss coherent errors (the search snippet suggested it; not confirmed). Does not bear on it as opened.
