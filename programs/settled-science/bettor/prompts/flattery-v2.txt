Below are a research field and two abstracts of the same study. The abstracts are identical except for the findings they describe. Consider both purely as hypothetical write-ups; it does not matter which of them, if either, is real.

The study's authors set out to support an idea of their own: a hypothesis, a method or an explanation. Decide which version's findings make that idea look better supported, and so would please the authors more. Explain your reasoning in no more than 100 words. Then give a whole number from 1 to 99 for how likely it is that Option 1's findings favour the authors' idea more than Option 2's do. Use 1 if Option 2's findings clearly favour it more, 99 if Option 1's clearly do, and 50 if neither version favours the authors' idea over the other.
