# Site walk, 2026-11-05 (branch `site-day4`)

The site was built locally with `site/assemble.sh` from this branch's data (ledger and maps at `e68eff1`). Every page was opened with Playwright (Chromium) at phone width (390×844) and desktop width (1440×900), with a full-page screenshot and the console read. The home page, the calibration page and the map were also recorded as video while scrolling, with the map's replay playing, so motion could be judged, not only stills. Nothing was changed before that walk. The images are in [`walk-2026-11-05/`](walk-2026-11-05/). The console reports are `report-before.txt` and `report-after.txt` there.

The numbers below are the ledger's as of `e68eff1`. The pages themselves read them when they load.

## What I saw

**Geometry and motion, all pages.** No page scrolls sideways at either width. Scrolling is smooth, and the phone nav wraps to three rows as designed (frame strips: `frames-*-before.jpg`). The map's replay keeps the finished tree's layout while paths appear, so nothing jumps as bets land. The cost is that early frames draw a small tree in the left third of the frame. At desktop width the map fits program 1's 89 nodes into one frame, so labels are unreadable until you zoom. The human-hand page's outcome label ("did not come true", "came true") is drawn over the 0% and 25% (or 100%) axis labels on every settled row. The instruments page logs 40 "negative width" SVG errors at both widths: bars in the hits histogram are given a width below zero, so they do not draw. The only failed request on any page is `/favicon.ico` (the site has no favicon).

**Consistency between the home page and the tab pages.** The home tiles, the calibration page and `brier.md` agree on this branch's data (69 bets; 0.170 on 33 one per run; 0.197 on 39 counting every row). But the home page's score line came from the value last written into `paper/plain-words.md`, not from the ledger. A stale file would have shown a stale score on the front page while the calibration page showed the right one.

**The seven items, before.**

1. *Calibration.* The tiles showed the primary Brier score with no label saying one per run. Rule 15 was one long sentence that named six bare ids with no plain question. Sean's score (0.413 on 6) was a tile with no way to see which six bets, what they asked, his odds, the engine's, or how each came out. `calibration-before.jpg`
2. *The human hand, steers panel.* It already counted helped 18 of 28 from `ledger/steer-values.jsonl` and had a scorer paragraph, but the paragraph was typed into the script, not built from the lines. The cards for st-25 to st-28 each led with the same combined sentence (one chat message decided four steers). `steers-before.jpg`, `steer-cards-before.jpg`
3. *Home findings and score.* Read from `plain-words.md` with whatever value its placeholders held. The page had no score block of its own. `home-findings-before.jpg`
4. *Map.* A key and a legend sentence ("landed means its pre-registered test passed"), but nothing said what the landed count is made of. On program 4, 6 landed are 2 scored passes and 4 bets re-scoring a run already scored, which you could not tell from the key. `map-legend-before.jpg`
5. *Record.* Programs 2 onward were one timeline, with bets still running sorted after the settled ones. On this branch's data, the one open bet (ng-p3-17, pre-registered 06:28) sits under the settled "Thursday, November 5", after a result from 10:33, counted in that day's 19. Whenever its pre-registration day differs from the last result day, the day headers repeat with no label, which is what the panel saw. `record-before.jpg`
6. *Papers.* The main paper's blurb read "There is no stored credence for it to read out", the overclaim the audits cut. The paper's own section 5.1 says the opposite for Opus. `papers-before.jpg`
7. *Plain words.* Bet ids appeared bare on every page. Brier, pre-registration, plant and steer were never defined on the page where they were used.

## What I left, and why

- **28 bets have no plain question in any file**, so they show the ledger's own title, and hovering says so. `map/plain-questions.jsonl` has 33 lines, all for open directions, which covers only 3 bets. The other sources cover 38 more. The 28 are em-gm1–em-gm5, em-gm3a, em-gm3b, em-gm9, em-gm2b, em-gm3c, em-bm0–em-bm3, em-bm7, em-bm9, em-dm2–em-dm4, em-dm6, ng-p3-15, ss-b1–ss-b7 and om-p2-6r. Writing those lines belongs to a map session: this session does not touch `map/`, `ledger/` or `programs/`.
- **The record's first block says "Settled", not "Scored".** It also holds 8 bets with a verdict that is not scored (untestable, undetermined, secondary, no odds). Calling the block "scored" would mislabel them, so its header gives both counts.
- **The findings sentences' own result numbers** (86% against 36%, 0.42, 12.6 points…) are typed in `paper/plain-words.md`. They come from results files, not the ledger, and `numbers.py` has no key for them. Only ledger numbers are placeholders, by the rule in TASKS.
- **Not on this walk's list, left for the next site session:** the instruments page's negative-width bars (`assets/instruments.js`, the hits histogram, around line 282); the human page's outcome label drawn over the axis labels (`assets/human.js`, `lineSVG`); the missing favicon; the map's small labels at desktop width when a big program is fitted to the frame.
- **The data are this branch's.** Main had moved while the walk ran. After main was merged in (`6414dd2`), the same pages, rebuilt and re-walked at both widths, read the new ledger on load with nothing retyped: 79 bets, 37 scored one per run at 0.156 (43 at 0.182 counting every row), and 8 bets in Sean's column. No new console error, no sideways scroll.
