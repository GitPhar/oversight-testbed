Calibration

Before each test, the engine wrote down how likely it was to work. Each mark below is one of those bets, shaped by what decided it: another model, the quantum chip, or the published record. Its place across is the odds it was given. Marks on the top line came true, and marks on the bottom line did not. If the odds were honest, bets given 20% would come true about one time in five, and the grey diamonds would sit on the diagonal.
