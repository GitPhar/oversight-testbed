A testbed for AI-driven research

An AI maps every candidate path and bets on them with stated odds, a human steers, an independent auditor tries to break every result, and both the AI and the human are scored on their judgment.

1 What this is

An AI that does research, and a public record of whether its judgment can be trusted.

Before every experiment, the AI writes down how likely it thinks the experiment is to work. Then it runs the cheapest test that could prove it wrong. A fresh AI session that has not seen the work tries to break the result. Each guess is scored against what happened, and the wrong guesses stay on the record.

2 What the human does

One person steers, from the cockpit only; the AI never asks them anything in a chat. On any open question, the human can write down their own odds before seeing the AI's number, and a bet goes ahead without them if they don't. They can veto a direction, giving a reason, or add a question the AI did not think of.

Every one of those moves is logged, and later scored, so the record shows whether the human's hand made the research better or worse.

3 What the quantum chip is for, and not for

One program uses a real IBM quantum chip as a referee. When the AI bets on how the chip will behave, the chip settles it. Nothing the AI writes can talk it round.

  • For: grading the AI's bets with outcomes set by physics, not by another AI.
  • Not for: computing anything, speeding anything up, or making the AI smarter. Every circuit is small enough to simulate on a laptop.

    The mapEvery path held open, and which ones the bets collapsed to. CockpitThe next questions to run, each asked one plain way: would you spend this, and will it be a hit? CalibrationThe odds we stated before data, against what happened. Kill logEvery direction that died, and why. The recordEach test in the order it ran. PapersThe write-ups, as readable pages.

    The five commitments

    1. Odds before data.Every bet carries the engine's odds and the human's odds, recorded separately, before any data are seen.
    2. Cheapest falsifier first.Archives before API, API before hardware; budgets in code; stop rules; stop-on-refusal.
    3. No result without audit.An independent session that has not seen the work tries to break every result, and its report is published whether or not the result stands.
    4. Nothing disappears.Every dead direction stays on the map with its diagnosis.
    5. Full reads first, all fields.Every program starts from adjacent research read in full, its "what to do next" extracted and filtered by cost and falsifiability.
