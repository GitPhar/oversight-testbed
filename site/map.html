How to read it

Each program starts from one question on the left. Paths fan out to the right, one per direction we could take. Faint dotted paths are still open. A bright green path is a bet that landed, and the next paths grow from it. A cross is a direction that died. Hover over or tap any point to see why. Press Replay to watch the paths open and collapse in the order the bets landed.
